Reference:

## Why credit matters here
We’re intentionally separating:
- *How to do a task* (build, deploy, debug) from
- *Why the system works this way* (engine contracts, hard caps) from
- *Stable reference* (env vars, file layout)

That split makes it possible to keep moving even while parts of the pipeline are still experimental.
